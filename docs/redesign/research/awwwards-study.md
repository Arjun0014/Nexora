## How to read the site blocks

- All measurements were taken in headless Chromium at 1440x900 (desktop) and 390x844 (phone) on 2026-09-21.
- `vw` values are computed font-size divided by viewport width. `vh` page lengths are `scrollHeight / 900`.
- Tracking is given in em (negative = tighter). `lh` = line-height as a multiple of font-size.
- Colours are computed `background-color` values or pixel samples from screenshots (marked "px").
- "SOTD" = Awwwards Site of the Day. Scores are the Awwwards jury score out of 10 as shown on the detail page.

## Site blocks

### 1. Aspen Search — https://www.aspensearch.com/
1. **What / award**: Executive-search (recruitment) firm for quant finance and tech. SOTD 15 Sep 2026, 7.48. Next.js + Lenis. Closest sector match to Nexora in the whole study.
2. **Pacing**: 12.1 vh desktop, 17.6 vh phone. 7 scenes: hero grid (2.0 vh), positioning + service cells (2.2), clients (1.9), testimonials (1.6), team (2.3), giant-type CTA (1.0), footer (0.9). Never a full-bleed "band": every scene is a set of cells in one continuous grid. It breathes by leaving entire grid cells empty (white), not by adding vertical padding.
3. **Type**: one family, Suisse Intl at weight 450, plus Suisse Intl Mono 400 for labels. Display 180.7 px = 12.55 vw, lh 1.0; section words ("Clients", "Team") same size; uppercase statement lines 150 px = 10.43 vw at lh 0.82; h3 37.9 px = 2.63 vw lh 1.1; body 16 px lh 1.3; mono labels 12 px uppercase lh 1.1. Tracking is a flat -0.04 em (CSS `letter-spacing: -4%`) on everything, including body. Ladder: 12 / 14 / 16 / 24 / 31 / 38 / 80 / 108-181. Display is 11.3x body; nothing between 38 and 80 px, which is what makes the big type read as architecture rather than as "a heading".
4. **Grid**: 6 equal columns (240 px at 1440), zero outer margin, every cell edged by a 1 px #232323 hairline. Cells are square or 2:1. The header is a grid row too: logo cell, live local-time cell (LA/NYC clock), nav cell, theme toggle cell, dark "Contact" cell. Text sits top-left in its cell with ~12 px inset; large words bottom-left. Asymmetry comes from which cells are left empty.
5. **Colour**: #ffffff base, #e0e0e0 and #d9d9d9 grey cells, #232323 charcoal cells, one accent #a1ffcb (mint). Client brand colours (#004b87, #08225a, #009aa6, #262160) appear only inside the active client cell. No page-long grade: colour is distributed per cell like a checkerboard. All transitions are hard cuts on hairlines.
6. **Images**: no normal photography. The brand mark and landscapes are rendered as 1-bit halftone/dither fields on 5 canvases; team portraits are `filter: grayscale(1)` with a mint name plate under each. The dither is the texture that replaces grain.
7. **Transitions / scroll**: Lenis. 12 CSS `position: sticky` elements, 0 GSAP pin-spacers: the giant section words stick inside their cell while neighbouring cells scroll past. CTA scene: the words LET'S / START / A / CONVERSATION each sit in their own black, white or mint tile over the dither field.
8. **Cursor**: native cursor kept; hover feedback is cell-level (cell fills with mint or the client's brand colour, arrow nudges).
9. **Services / process / contact**: services are three charcoal cells stacked in one column, each = tiny mono index (01-03), 38 px title, one paragraph, a generative wireframe figure (overlapping circles, a rotating meridian sphere) and four mono tags with a mint square bullet. No icons, no cards with shadows. No process section. Footer is a cell grid: tagline cell ("Raise your trajectory"), nav cell, two city clocks, email cell, LinkedIn cell, a large charcoal logo cell and a mint "Let's start a conversation" cell crossed by a single diagonal arrow line.
10. **Mobile**: grid drops to 2 columns but keeps its hairlines; display rises to 20.7 vw uppercase at lh 0.82; header stays a grid row with a mint "Menu" cell; service cells stack; team becomes full-width B/W portrait + mint plate. Nothing is removed.
11. **Transferable principle**: a visible, strict modular grid with hairlines and deliberately empty cells is a complete art direction on its own; it reads as "institution" without a single proof point. Solves: *making a new company feel established without proof*, and *footer* (footer as a grid of useful cells: Doha local time, email, registration number, giant mark).
   Caveat for Nexora: Aspen leans on stats (500+, $1M-5M, 19+ years). Remove them and the grid still holds, which is the point.

### 2. Truck'N Roll — https://trucknroll.com/
1. **What / award**: Entertainment-logistics company (moves tours, festivals, film sets) by Locomotive. SOTD 3 Jun 2026, 7.23. Swup page transitions + Lenis + OGL. B2B service company talking to event producers: same buyer type as Nexora Events.
2. **Pacing**: 12.4 vh desktop, 10.9 vh phone. 9 scenes: header+hero (1.2 vh), "what we do" statement (0.8), MOVE SHOWS FORWARD type wall + three key steps (1.7), full-bleed photo with overlaid statements (1.1), use cases (1.0), bracketed giant statement (0.6), white fleet/stats block (2.6), ghost-type culture teaser (1.0), violet footer (1.6). Dense-sparse rhythm: every dense text scene is followed by a scene that is one sentence at 10-29 vw.
3. **Type**: National 2 Condensed 900 uppercase for display + Helvetica Now Display 500/700 for everything else. Display 420 px = 29.2 vw (single word "WE"), 232 px = 16.1 vw, h1 200 px = 13.9 vw, 152 px = 10.6 vw, all at lh 0.70 and -0.01 to -0.03 em. h2 72 px = 5.0 vw lh 0.9 -0.03 em; h3 51 px = 3.54 vw; nav 32 px bold (nav is set as large as an h4); body 16 px lh 1.1 (tight, short paragraphs of 3-4 lines max, column ~190 px wide); smallest 12 px. Ladder 12/13/14/16/17/20/32/51/72/152/200/232/420. Condensed black caps vs neutral grotesk = the whole identity.
4. **Grid**: 12 columns, gap 12 px, 16 px side padding (very thin margins, content runs nearly edge to edge). Text blocks are narrow (2 of 12 columns) and sit hard-left or at column 7; images are small (2-4 columns) with 8-14 px radius and placed off-axis next to large type. Step items zig-zag between column 1 and column 7, each topped by a full-width 1 px rule.
5. **Colour**: #131313 (hero through use cases, 0-7.2 vh) -> #fefefe (fleet/stats, 7.2-9.8 vh) -> #131313 (ghost-type teaser) -> #4e37ff electric violet footer. Secondary greys #d8d3d3, #5e6266. Photos are graded cold blue-black with sodium-orange highlights so they dissolve into #131313. Cuts are hard; the footer is the exception: the violet slab rises as tilted marquee bands that overlap the dark section (overlap transition).
6. **Images**: night-time, low-key trucking photography; tight detail crops (headlights, a mirror, a hand signal) at ~4:3 and 1:1 with 8-14 px radius; one full-bleed 16:9 scene with type laid over it. A small round violet play button sits on the hero video thumbnail. `mix-blend-mode: darken` used on a type layer.
7. **Transitions / scroll**: Lenis + `data-scroll` in-view classes (Locomotive's own scroll module), no GSAP pinning, 0 sticky elements. Ghost-type scene: a dot-matrix/halftone rendering of a sentence at 16 vw sits at ~8% contrast behind a tiny centred caption and a violet button. Footer: two counter-moving marquee bands, rotated ~-4 deg, repeating TOUR / IT'S A / logo lockups.
8. **Cursor**: native. Hover = arrow nudges and violet fills on buttons.
9. **Services / process / contact**: the "three key steps" are set as `(1) Flawless Execution`, `(2) Safety-first standards`, `(3) Solution oriented`: parenthesised numeral and title on one 51 px line under a hairline, 3-line paragraph, one small detail photo; items alternate left/right columns so the list reads as a staggered path, not a stack. Contact page = giant "LET'S MOVE YOUR NEXT SHOW." + one long form split into parenthesis-numbered groups ((1) Personal information, (2) Show details), transparent inputs 64 px tall, 20 px text, 12 px label above, segmented radio row for transport type, violet submit block. Footer repeats three doors: Move. / Join. / Social. each with a one-line explanation and one link. 404: "404. WRONG VENUE." in the same condensed caps with the violet footer sliding up underneath.
10. **Mobile**: display drops to 21 vw / 11.5 vw, body to 14.2 px; everything single column; main nav moves to a fixed bottom bar (Services, Culture, Contact); step images go full width; stats table keeps its hairline rows.
11. **Transferable principle**: alternate "reading" scenes with "shouting" scenes. One sentence set at 10-29 vw in condensed caps is a complete section; it needs no image and no proof. Solves: *why-contact-us commitments* (each commitment can be one shouted line) and *404*.

### 3. United Carriers — https://unitedcarriers.com/
1. **What / award**: Australian freight forwarder (air, ocean, customs, 3PL) by Bearplus. SOTD 6 Sep 2026, 7.28. Webflow + Lenis + Barba + OGL. Pure B2B operations company, no glamour product.
2. **Pacing**: 31.4 vh, the longest corporate page in the study, but only 9 scenes: hero (2.5 vh), intro (1.6), one pinned services film (13.5 vh!), "why" road scene (7.4), testimonials (3.3), partner grid (1.7), insights (0.9), FAQ (0.8), CTA + footer (1.6). 67% of the scroll length is two scrubbed scenes; the DOM content in between is short. Breathing = the white voids around a single CGI object.
3. **Type**: BT Steinhart 700 uppercase (squared geometric) for display, Helvetica Neue 400/500 for text, BT Steinhart Mono 400/500 for labels. Display is modest: h1/h2 80 px = 5.56 vw lh 1.05, tracking 0; statement 60 px = 4.17 vw; only the stat numerals reach 133 px = 9.26 vw. Body 13.3 px lh 1.8 (small, airy); labels 10 px and 8.3 px mono uppercase (8.3 px is the smallest type in the study and is too small). Two-tone headlines: first word at 40% grey, rest at #111111 ("RELIABILITY / AT EVERY MILESTONE").
4. **Grid**: 22-column grid (gap 16.7 px) and a 16-column variant, 20 px side padding. Text is confined to narrow 3-4 column blocks pushed to the far left or far right; the centre of the page is reserved for the moving object. Partner logos sit in a 5-column table drawn with corner "+" registration marks instead of full borders.
5. **Colour**: hero = deep ocean image, px #001d4c -> #ffffff (intro, service film on white) with a #111111 ground strip that rises to become the black services panel -> #ffffff "why" scene split by a #111111 vertical road -> ocean blue again (px #001a4a) -> #ffffff -> #eeeeee -> a violet-blue radial glow (#0016cb family) blooming from the bottom edge before the footer -> #ffffff footer. Accent #0016cb appears only as the glow and hover. The colour script is white with two returns to the hero's ocean: the hero world is revisited at 55% of the page.
6. **Images**: almost no photography. CGI vehicles (reach-stacker, truck, container ship, 747) rendered on pure white or top-down on water/cloud, played as scroll-scrubbed image sequences on canvas (6 canvases). Hero is a top-down close-up of coloured containers; at 16.7-22 vh the same shot pulls back to reveal the whole ship at sea with the headline over it. Ghost words ("OUR SERVICES") at ~6% grey sit behind the truck and are revealed as it drives past.
7. **Transitions / scroll**: Lenis; 16 sticky elements, no GSAP pin-spacers. Scene changes are made by an object, not a wipe: the truck drives off-screen left and the black ground strip it stood on rises to become the next section; the vertical road scene has the top-down truck travelling down the page at scroll speed; the plane flies through cloud to bridge into testimonials. `mix-blend-mode: difference` header so nav survives the white/black/blue changes.
8. **Cursor**: `data-cursor` follower present: small ring that scales on links; native cursor remains.
9. **Services / process / contact**: services are a horizontal row of 6 line icons + uppercase title + 3-line blurb on black, reached after the truck scene (this is an icon row, the weakest part of the site; the film before it does the persuading). "Why" = the road: a vertical black lane down the page centre, benefits alternate left and right of it as the truck passes (4 benefits over 7.4 vh). Contact = a black CTA bar, then a white footer with sitemap columns, payment marks, a scrolling list of industries served in grey type, office address, a dotted world map, and a giant dot-matrix wordmark bleeding off the bottom edge.
10. **Mobile**: not inspected.
11. **Transferable principle**: give the page a "continuity object" that travels through sections and causes the transitions (it drives off, the ground rises, the camera pulls back to reveal where we started). The hero world is revisited mid-page at a different scale, so the page feels like one film instead of a hero followed by a brochure. Solves: *colour and narrative continuity out of a dark cinematic hero* and *process without a numbered sticky list* (a path with stations left and right of a moving marker).

### 4. ERA Residence — https://www.era-residence.com/
1. **What / award**: 25-unit Mediterranean residential development, entirely CGI-rendered (nothing built yet) by The First The Last. SOTD 31 Aug 2026 and Site of the Month, 7.61 (highest score in this study). Webflow + GSAP ScrollTrigger + SplitText + Lenis + Barba. Relevant twice over: premium hospitality feel, and it sells something that does not exist yet using only renders.
2. **Pacing**: 27.3 vh desktop, 17.5 vh phone. ~14 scenes. The hero alone is 5.8 vh of pinned camera moves through one render (terrace -> pool -> title). After that the rhythm is strict: statement scene (1 vh, one centred sentence) -> image scene -> statement -> image. Dense information (floor plans, amenities list) is confined to two scenes of 3.3-5 vh each; everything else is one idea per viewport.
3. **Type**: three voices. Ambroise Francois (ultra-condensed high-contrast didone) uppercase for display: h1 172.8 px = 12.0 vw lh 0.87 -0.024 em; section titles 122 px = 8.5 vw; statements 36 px = 2.5 vw lh 1.0. Sloop Script for one or two words only ("Estepona", "live in", "Book a call") at 108 px = 7.5 vw, overlapping the serif. Maison Neue Extended for text: body 11.7 px lh 1.38 -0.024 em; labels 9.9 px bold uppercase +0.048 em; smallest 8.1 px bold uppercase +0.32 em ("Menu"). Ladder is brutal: 8 / 10 / 12 / 36 / 57 / 108 / 122 / 173. There is no "normal" 16-24 px text at all: you either read captions or monuments.
4. **Grid**: 10-column base (gap 14.4 px, side padding 43 px), with 8/6/5/4 sub-grids. Composition is centre-axis: statements are centred in a ~4-column measure with a small brand glyph under them; images alternate between full-bleed and a small centred 4:3 thumbnail with slider ticks. A thin vertical rule + section number runs down the left margin as a progress spine.
5. **Colour**: three colours rotate, all sampled from the renders: aubergine #340c24 (wine/bougainvillea shadow), sky #b5cedb, stone #f3f3ec; text navy #17233b. Page order: #340c24 hero (0-5.8 vh, mostly covered by imagery) -> #b5cedb (4.0-7.0) -> #f3f3ec (7.0-12.0) -> #340c24 (12.0-13.4) -> #b5cedb (13.4-15.4) -> #f3f3ec (15.4-17.7) -> #340c24 (17.7-18.7) -> #f3f3ec (20.2-24.8) -> #340c24 footer (24.8-27.3). So dark is a recurring anchor that appears four times, never as "the dark half". Seams are hidden three ways: a rising arch-shaped clip (720 px radius semicircle of the next colour growing from the bottom edge), cut-out bougainvillea PNGs that overlap the seam in the foreground, and full-bleed images placed exactly on the boundary.
6. **Images**: warm daylight CGI, saturated sky and magenta flowers; full-bleed 16:9, portrait 3:4 insets, small 4:3 slider thumbs. Cut-out foliage in the corners parallaxes faster than the content (`data-parallax`). A cloud layer slides over the aerial shot. 8 short looping videos bring renders to life (water, leaves). "ARCHITECTURE" word sits half behind the building (type-behind-image depth).
7. **Transitions / scroll**: Lenis + ScrollTrigger; `clip` class on most sections = clip-path reveals; arch wipe between chapters; hero camera push; horizontal drag gallery ("drag to see more"). Barba page transitions.
8. **Cursor**: native cursor; a circular outlined "View available apartments" badge (approx 60 px ring with 8 px caps text) follows/anchors near images as the primary hover affordance.
9. **Services / process / contact**: amenities are a plain stacked list of serif caps lines (GATED COMMUNITY / SWIMMING POOL / PARKING AREA ...) where the active line is full white and the others 40% opacity while the background image changes: a list that swaps the scene, no icons. Timeline/status is a centred stack: "DEVELOPER / SALES & MARKETING / LICENSE OBTAINED / 2026" with superscript markers. Contact: aubergine footer where the phone number itself is the display type (122 px serif), address in 10 px caps under it; booking form is a modal (title, name, email, phone, message; white 38 px inputs, 1 px #ccc underline, no radius).
10. **Mobile**: display goes up to 23 vw; body 12.2 px; script overlaps preserved; centre-axis compositions survive untouched because they were already single-column; hero camera move replaced by a static crop with "A PLACE / TO RETURN TO" split left and right of the frame; amenities list overlays the image.
11. **Transferable principle**: derive the section colours from the imagery (sample three colours out of the renders) and let a dark anchor colour recur 3-4 times down the page; hide each seam with an overlapping foreground element or a shaped clip. Solves: *colour continuity out of a dark cinematic hero* and *evolving grade instead of black-then-white*. For Nexora the five world colours (amber, violet, teal, steel/orange, navy/cyan) plus the studio bronze are the sampling source.

Map scene shows departure time, distance and temperature as a data card beside a rendered continent. 10. **Mobile**: not inspected.
11. **Transferable principle**: credibility from specifics, not statistics: a signed founder's letter, coordinates, departure times, a named place. Solves: *what Nexora is / making a new company feel established without proof*.

### 7. Lama Lama — https://lamalama.com/

The research agent above reached seven sites before its session hit a rate limit. I opened a further batch
