# LOCKED / STRONGLY DECIDED

## Business direction
- Company: Nexora Hospitality and Services W.L.L.
- Qatar
- Workforce / staffing / services direction
- Competitor reference: Wings
- Construction excluded

## Hero concept
- circular five-sector diorama
- centered master overview
- high 3/4 top-down opening
- tall radial barriers
- open tops / open wedge fronts
- five distinct sector palettes
- close camera looks outside → inward
- right-side human action
- left-side copy space
- time-freeze concept
- no visible neighboring sectors in close scenes

## Hero sectors
1. Hospitality
2. Events & Promotions
3. Facilities & Support
4. Specialist / Technical Workforce
5. Recruitment & Workforce Management

## Opening idle
- 15-second seamless loop
- rigid 360° clockwise diorama rotation
- fixed camera

## Sector transitions
- motion briefly resumes
- right divider wall wipes viewport
- next sector appears
- action moves
- abrupt freeze
- roughly 5 seconds
