# Interaction Idea Bank

These are optional ingredients for Claude Design / Astra.

Nothing here is required except the already-defined hero.

Choose only interactions that improve the story or usability.

---

## 1. Cinematic restrained opening

Reference:
- Arc'teryx System 0
- Lambert | Lambert

Idea:
Let the diorama dominate the first impression.

Avoid immediately surrounding it with:
- KPI cards
- multiple CTAs
- navigation panels
- long copy
- decorative UI

Use restraint.

---

## 2. Sector-responsive website palette

Reference:
- Love + Money

Idea:
As the hero enters each world, parts of the website could inherit that world's palette.

Examples:
- small accent line
- page background wash
- section number
- cursor
- progress indicator
- headline accent
- subtle ambient glow

Do not repaint every UI element aggressively.

---

## 3. Large typography as a transition mask

Reference:
- supplied Pinterest video

Idea:
A word or headline scales far beyond the viewport and masks a section change.

Potential use:
- hero exit
- Services introduction
- transition between major site chapters

Could combine:
- type scaling
- background zoom
- brief blur
- incoming scene resolving into focus

Use sparingly.

---

## 4. Multi-use contextual cursor

Reference:
- Noho

Possible states:
- SCROLL
- DRAG
- VIEW
- EXPLORE
- APPLY
- REQUEST

Potential uses:
- hero navigation feedback
- service card drag hint
- project / industry exploration
- CTA states

Rules:
- must communicate something
- should not be decorative noise
- disable or simplify on touch
- respect reduced motion

---

## 5. Swipe / Tinder-style service cards

Reference:
- LxL Creative

Potential use:
- services
- sectors
- staffing categories

Possible behavior:
- cards stacked with depth
- drag left/right
- active card reveals details
- sector palette changes with card
- cursor changes to DRAG

Alternative:
horizontal drag carousel with physical inertia.

Accessibility:
- keyboard buttons
- visible labels
- touch support
- content accessible without drag

---

## 6. Chapter numbering

Reference:
- Love + Money
- Arc'teryx system stages

Possible use:
- 01 Hospitality
- 02 Events
- 03 Facilities
- 04 Technical
- 05 Workforce

or outside the hero for site chapters.

This can make a long site feel intentional rather than like disconnected sections.

Not required.

---

## 7. Hero exit as a deliberate moment

Current problem to solve:
The cinematic hero cannot simply stop and become a generic web page.

Options Claude Design can explore:

### A. Huge-type zoom
Use Pinterest-style scale transition.

### B. Pull back into editorial grid
Cinematic frame shrinks into a content module.

### C. Color collapse
Current sector color drains into the main website palette.

### D. Hard graphic chapter break
A confident oversized section title deliberately ends the filmic experience.

Choose one, not all.

---

## 8. Scroll progress as world navigation

Potential subtle UI:
- five dots
- five numbers
- five sector names
- radial progress motif

Should indicate:
- current sector
- progress
- possibility of additional worlds

Do not compete with the scene.

---

## 9. Freeze / resume micro-language

The time-freeze idea could potentially continue in smaller ways outside the hero.

Examples:
- cards pause on hover
- micro-video freezes when pointer enters
- typography catches mid-motion
- image sequence briefly “stops time”

Use carefully.

The hero should remain the strongest expression of this idea.

---

## 10. One interaction per moment

This is the most important principle.

Bad:
- draggable card
- morphing cursor
- animated background
- giant type
- scroll parallax
- video
- color transition

all happening at once.

Better:
Each major moment has one dominant behavior.

The site should alternate:
- motion
- stillness
- information
- interaction
- conversion
