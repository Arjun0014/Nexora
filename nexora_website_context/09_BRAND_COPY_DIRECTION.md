# Brand and Copy Direction

There is no complete supplied brand guide yet.

Claude Design should create a coherent visual language around the established hero.

---

# Desired brand feeling

Nexora should feel:

- premium
- modern
- operationally capable
- human
- confident
- GCC-relevant
- refined
- serious without looking bureaucratic

It should NOT feel like:

- a generic manpower agency template
- a construction contractor
- a cheap recruitment portal
- a nightclub brand
- a futuristic sci-fi dashboard
- a luxury brand using gold everywhere for no reason

---

# Visual anchor

The hero already establishes:

- dark premium studio space
- bronze / architectural material
- miniature world-building
- cinematic lighting
- distinct sector palettes
- time-freeze imagery

The rest of the site should feel related without copying the diorama into every section.

---

# Working concept line

The project has been developed under:

> **One World, Many Workforces**

This is a strong internal creative concept and may work publicly.

It is not mandatory final copy.

Claude Design may propose a better headline if it preserves the idea.

---

# Copy voice

Use:
- concise statements
- active language
- operational clarity
- short confident headings
- concrete service language

Avoid:
- “leading company” without evidence
- “best”
- “world-class” as filler
- “trusted by hundreds” without evidence
- fake metrics
- long corporate mission paragraphs
- generic AI-style phrases
- repetitive “we are committed to excellence” copy

---

# Copy should explain outcomes

Better:
> Flexible hospitality teams when operations scale.

Worse:
> We provide comprehensive innovative hospitality manpower solutions of the highest quality.

Better:
> Source. Screen. Deploy. Support.

Worse:
> Our end-to-end synergistic process ensures optimal manpower outcomes.

---

# Human focus

The site should communicate that workforce is about people, not inventory.

Good themes:
- the right people
- the right moment
- smooth operations
- coordinated teams
- service quality
- human capability

Avoid treating workers as anonymous units.

---

# Language

The legacy site had EN / AR behavior, but bilingual requirements for Nexora are not confirmed.

Claude Design may plan for Arabic readiness if sensible, but do not assume Arabic content exists.

If Arabic is later required:
- support RTL properly
- do not rely on mirrored visuals that break the hero composition
